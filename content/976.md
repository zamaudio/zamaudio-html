## Get zam-plugins now! ($0)

zam-plugins is a collection of LV2/LADSPA/VST/JACK audio plugins for sound processing developed in-house at ZamAudio. The default settings and almost every slider is calibrated to standard ranges.

Installation of lv2 is a manual process by unzipping the plugins and saving the bundle in a designated path. The paths are documented [here](https://lv2plug.in/pages/filesystem-hierarchy-standard.html) for all operating systems. macOS has a pkg installer instead on releases below.

## zam-plugins for GNU/Linux, Windows and macOS:

### <https://github.com/zamaudio/zam-plugins/releases>

For those who can’t wait to get their hands on the source code, it is also available on [github](https://github.com/zamaudio/zam-plugins).

## ZaMaximX2

One of the best plugins in the series, this acts as a brickwall limiter for mastering in its default state, but can also be tweaked to raise the average level as a stereo maximizer without ever clipping. Sounds musical even with rough treatment. (Based on DSP found in a DAFX’02 paper).

![zamaximx2](/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/zamaximx2.png)

## ZamAutoSat

An automatic saturation plugin, has been known to provide smooth levelling to live mic channels.  
You can apply this plugin generously without affecting the tone.

![zamautosat](/wp-content/uploads/2014/10/zamautosat.png)

## ZamComp

A powerful mono compressor strip. Adds real beef to a kick or snare drum with the right settings.

![zamcomp](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/zamcomp.png)

## ZamCompX2

Stereo version of ZamComp with knee slew control.

![zamcompx2](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/zamcompx2.png)

## ZamEQ2

Two band parametric equaliser with high and low shelving circuits.

![zameq2](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/zameq2.png)

## ZamGEQ31

31 band graphic equaliser, good for eq of live spaces, removing unwanted noise from a track etc.

![zamgeq31](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/zamgeq31.png)

## ZamHeadX2

HRTF acoustic filtering plugin for directional sound.

![zamheadx2](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/zamheadx2.png)

## ZamPhono

A collection of phono filters for restoring vinyl records, or preparing to cut new ones.

![zamphono](/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/zamphono.png)

## ZamGate

Gate plugin for ducking low gain sounds.

![zamgate](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/zamgate.png)

## ZamGateX2

Gate plugin for ducking low gain sounds, stereo version.

![zamgatex2](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/zamgatex2.png)

## ZamTube

Wicked distortion effect. Wave digital filter physical model of a triode tube amplifier stage, with modelled tone stacks from real guitar amplifiers (thanks D. Yeh et al).

![zamtube](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/zamtube.png)

## ZamDelay

A simple feedback delay unit with sync-to-host BPM feature and filter.

![zamdelay](/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/zamdelay.png)

## ZamDynamicEQ

A dynamic equalizer that changes its gain based on detecting a narrow band of frequencies. Can be tuned to alter other frequency ranges on-the-fly.

![zamdynamiceq](/wp-content/uploads/2016/11/zamdynamiceq.png)

## ZaMultiComp

Mono multiband compressor, with 3 adjustable bands.

![zamulticomp](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/zamulticomp.png)

## ZaMultiCompX2

Flagship of zam-plugins:  
Stereo version of ZaMultiComp, with individual threshold controls for each band and real-time visualisation of comp curves.

_Ever since I got the ZaMultiCompX2 plugin, I have never used (other) compressor. It has independent gain control attack and release with knee makes it versatile and superior. You can make it a dinosaurs and eat up annoying frequency and use it as a way of EQ._

![zamulticompx2](/wp-content/uploads/2016/06/zamulticompx2.png)
